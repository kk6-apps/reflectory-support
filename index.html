Reflectory — Support

Reflectory is a private journal for the books and films you read and watch.

Questions, feedback, or trouble with the app? Email us at kk6.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Privacy Policy

  • Reflectory is built to keep your data private.
  • All your entries, reflections, and notes are stored on your own device and, if you enable it, synced through your own iCloud account. We do not operate any servers, we do not collect your data, and we never have access to what you write.
  • Your data is never transmitted to us or to any third party for storage. iCloud sync, if enabled, happens entirely within your own Apple account.
  • The app uses The Movie Database (TMDB) and Open Library to look up book and film information. These lookups send only your search terms to those services to return results; they are not linked to your identity.
  • If you contact us by email, we use your email address only to reply to you.
  • Because we store nothing, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or lose.
